# Agentic Textbook — Devpost creative directions

All assets use a 3:2 landscape ratio. The generated variants were created with the built-in image-generation tool and then copied into the project without overwriting the existing submission thumbnail.

## Recommended pairing

- Lead gallery image: `hero-directions/hero-01-product-proof.png`
- Thumbnail: `thumbnail-directions/thumbnail-03-folded-shared-surface.png`

The lead image proves that the product exists and makes the WebMCP relationship visible. The sculptural thumbnail behaves like a distinctive project identity rather than a miniature screenshot.

### 02 — Tactile shared surface

`hero-directions/hero-02-tactile-shared-surface.png`

Final prompt:

> A prize-caliber 3:2 editorial photograph in which an AI-agent conversation visibly becomes a tactile, rearrangeable lesson beside it. A dark display in the background retains the broad Codex-chat/cream-learning-canvas structure; an open cream notebook in the foreground contains removable lesson sections. Deep forest table, soft window light, real paper fibre, no headline, fake readable UI, trophies, robots, brains, holograms, or glossy AI effects.

## Selection logic

- Choose hero 01 when credibility and proof matter most.
- Choose hero 02 when you want the most editorial, human, anti-slop direction.
- Choose hero 03 when you want maximum campaign energy and immediate memorability.
- Choose thumbnail 03 for the strongest premium identity.
- Choose thumbnail 04 for the clearest explanation at a glance.
- Choose thumbnail 01 for the most human and tactile signal.
